GAMING & CINEMATIC

Assassin's Creed

A large-scale live performance bringing gaming and cinematic music into the concert environment.

Assassin's Creed brought together the worlds of gaming, cinematic music and large-scale live performance in a concert environment.

For Ptolemaios, the project was particularly significant because of the breadth of instruments involved. Rather than contributing through a single instrumental identity, he performed as a multi-instrumentalist, moving between different instruments and musical colours throughout the performance.

This demanded a different kind of musicianship. The focus was not on arranging or producing the music, but on being able to move convincingly between different instrumental roles and become part of a much larger cinematic sound world.

The performance took place in the context of Gamescom, bringing the music of Assassin's Creed to a live audience on a scale very different from a conventional concert.

One performance. Multiple instruments. One cinematic world.

The multi-instrumentalist role was at the heart of the experience.
