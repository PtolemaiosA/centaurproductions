Challe

A collaboration with Suhaib Ehsan exploring the meeting point between songwriting, musicianship and contemporary production.

Challe is a Rock & Pop project created through collaboration with Suhaib Ehsan.

The project grew from the idea that a song can remain direct and emotionally accessible while still leaving room for character, texture and musical detail.

Rather than treating production as something separate from the song itself, the process focused on finding the right musical environment for the composition and allowing the performances to retain their individuality.

Working together made the project a dialogue between different musical perspectives. The result sits comfortably between the immediacy of a song-driven Rock & Pop production and the more detailed approach that comes from a musician-led collaboration.

The goal was simple: keep the song at the centre while giving it a sound and identity of its own.
